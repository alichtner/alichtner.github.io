**Distance:** ~6.5 mi · **Elevation gain:** 1,200 ft · **Drive:** ~2 hr  
**Near:** Index, WA · **Pass:** Northwest Forest Pass  
[WTA](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/hikes/tonga-ridge)

---

A gorgeous fall hike along a high ridge with sweeping views of the surrounding Cascades. The trail winds through open meadows and forest before reaching the summit of Mount Sawyer, where the views open up in every direction.

We got engaged at the top.

WTA lists this as 8 miles but it's noticeably shorter — closer to 6.5. The trailhead is easy to find off US-2 past Skykomish. Northwest Forest Pass required.
