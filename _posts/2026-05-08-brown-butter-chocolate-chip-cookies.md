**Category:** Desserts &middot; Cookies  
**Recipe:** [NYT &mdash; Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024108-brown-butter-chocolate-chip-cookies)  
**Rating:** 4 / 5 &middot; **Make again:** Yes &middot; **Made by:** Jamel

---

**Process notes.** Easy to bake, no need for room temperature butter (love that), and the wet and dry come together fast. They spread nicely when baking. This recipe yielded 24 cookies.

**Tasting notes.** Well balanced &mdash; crunchy edges, soft chewy interior, and bigger chocolate chunks are definitely better. The browned butter gives a deep richness that elevates the brown sugar. Used coarse kosher salt instead of what the recipe calls for, which gave a better burst of salt throughout each bite.

**For next time.** Used a semi-sweet and milk chocolate from a nearby specialty store. Next time: increase the brown sugar and salt, and go 70% milk chocolate / 30% semi-sweet.
