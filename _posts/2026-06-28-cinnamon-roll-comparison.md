A semi-scientific comparison of cinnamon roll recipes. Each roll is made from a different recipe and scored by the same tasters.

---

### Roll #1 &mdash; Ambitious Kitchen

**Recipe:** [Ambitious Kitchen &mdash; Best Cinnamon Rolls](https://www.ambitiouskitchen.com/best-cinnamon-rolls/)  
**Notes:** Made with pride colors.

| Taster | Score | Notes |
|--------|------:|-------|
| Aaron | 7 / 10 | Nice soft texture with some chew, nice cinnamon level. I miss the slight crunch or caramelization that can happen with cinnamon rolls. It's almost too fluffy. The cream cheese could benefit from a bit of acid. |
| Jamel | 8 / 10 | Perfect chew and tear, flavor was balanced with cinnamon and sugar. Could use more tang in the icing. |
| David | 9 / 10 | Really wracking my brain trying to think of anything I dislike about this cinnamon roll. |
| Ellie | 9.5 / 10 | Literally cannot think of a single critique, she's perfect. Slight preference for icing over cream cheese frosting, but damn the cream cheese frosting is good too. |
