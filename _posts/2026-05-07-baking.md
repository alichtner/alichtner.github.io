### Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies — May 8, 2026

**Category:** Desserts &middot; Cookies  
**Recipe:** [NYT - Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024108-brown-butter-chocolate-chip-cookies)  
**Rating:** 4 / 5 &middot; **Make again:** Yes  
**Who Made it:** Jamel

**Process notes.** Easy to bake, no need to have room temperature butter (love that),  the wet and dry come together fast. They spread nicely when baking.  This recipe yielded 24 cookies. 

**Tasting notes.**  The cookies are well balanced with crunchy edges and a soft chewy interior, also the bigger the chocolate chunks the better. The browned butter gives the cookie a deep richness elevating the brown sugar.. I also used coarse kosher salt instead of what the recipe calls for, which gave a better burst of salt throughout each bite. 


**For next time.** Thanks to a specialty grocery store in walking distance I was able to acquire very nice chocolate, so I used a smile-sweet and a milk chocolate for these cookies. Next time I am going to increase the brown sugar, the salt and go 70% milk chocolate and 30% semi-sweet.

### Our Favorite Sandwich Bread — May 6, 2026

**Category:** Bread &middot; White Bread  
**Recipe:** [King Arthur — Our Favorite Sandwich Bread](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/our-favorite-sandwich-bread-recipe)  
**Rating:** 4 / 5 &middot; **Make again:** Yes  
**Who Made it:** Aaron


**Process notes.** Easy and quick! The loaf came out a bit small.

**Tasting notes.** Really soft and lovely. Lightly sweet with a nice smell — reminds me of Grandma's challah when toasted.

**For next time.** Increase the recipe size by 50%, or check that the yeast is more active.

### Vermont Sourdough — April 20, 2026

**Category:** Bread &middot; Sourdough  
**Recipe:** [King Arthur — Vermont Sourdough](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/vermont-sourdough-recipe)  
**Rating:** 3 / 5 &middot; **Make again:** Yes  
**Who Made it:** Aaron

**Process notes.** Recipe was pretty involved. Next time I'd bake it a bit darker.

**Tasting notes.** Good flavor. The crumb came out a bit tighter than I expected, but it toasts up well and has a pleasant texture once toasted.

**Modifications.** None this time.
