## Bakes

### Our Favorite Sandwich Bread — May 6, 2026

**Category:** Bread &middot; White Bread  
**Recipe:** [King Arthur — Our Favorite Sandwich Bread](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/our-favorite-sandwich-bread-recipe)  
**Rating:** 4 / 5 &middot; **Make again:** Yes

**Process notes.** Easy and quick! The loaf came out a bit small.

**Tasting notes.** Really soft and lovely. Lightly sweet with a nice smell — reminds me of Grandma's challah when toasted.

**For next time.** Increase the recipe size by 50%, or check that the yeast is more active.

### Vermont Sourdough — April 20, 2026

**Category:** Bread &middot; Sourdough  
**Recipe:** [King Arthur — Vermont Sourdough](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/vermont-sourdough-recipe)  
**Rating:** 3 / 5 &middot; **Make again:** Yes

**Process notes.** Recipe was pretty involved. Next time I'd bake it a bit darker.

**Tasting notes.** Good flavor. The crumb came out a bit tighter than I expected, but it toasts up well and has a pleasant texture once toasted.

**Modifications.** None this time.
