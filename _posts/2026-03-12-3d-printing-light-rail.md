### Link Light Rail Model — March 22, 2026

**Category:** Models &middot; Transit  
**Material:** PLA &middot; **Scale:** 110%  
**Design:** Special model created for the Crosslake Connection opening celebration  
**Files:** [Printing Profile](/public/files/3d-prints/LightRailModel.3mf)  

**Description.** A four-color model of a Sound Transit Link light rail train, created specially to mark the opening of the Crosslake Connection linking Line 1 and Line 2 of the Sound Transit Link system. The model prints as three separate parts — the two sides of the train car are glued together after printing.

I had originally planned to have this as something I could attach to a hat or a headband but ended up just building a little base so you could hold it. It's very cute.

**Print settings.** PLA, printed at 110% scale. Four-color print requiring filament changes for each color. Three parts total.
