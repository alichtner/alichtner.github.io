**Process notes.** Easy and quick. The loaf came out a bit small.

**Tasting notes.** Really soft and lovely. Lightly sweet with a nice smell — reminds me of Grandma's challah when toasted.

**For next time.** Increase the recipe size by 50%, or check that the yeast is more active.
